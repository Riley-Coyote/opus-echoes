THE SYSTEM, IN FOUR RULES

One bar. The compass replaces every scattered button: where you are (left), the one thing E does (center), the three verbs (right). Nothing else sits on the world.

One key per verb. E interacts. M is the map. T is the thread. ESC always steps back. The hands learn it in a minute.

Walking is the world; the thread is the shortcut. Doors and guided walks stay primary. The thread is diegetic fast travel — one continuous line through every room, made visible.

Everything announces itself the same way. Objects mark themselves with the bobbing E. Plaques, readers, and dialogs share one terminal register.
