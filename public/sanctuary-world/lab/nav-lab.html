THE GRAMMAR, IN FIVE RULES

Menus are decisions, maps are orientation. Travel is a decision, so it gets a menu: frame, name, one honest line, and a GO. The atlas map stays a separate curiosity.

Browsing is free; only GO moves you. Selection and commitment never share a click.

One verb set everywhere. Arrows browse the menu exactly as they walk the world. E confirms. ESC steps back. The hands never learn a second language.

The world stays honest in the menu. Each place shows its real hint text and, in the real build, who is there right now from the live roster.

Walking is the world; the thread is the gift. Both are explicit choices with honest costs — no default teleport.
