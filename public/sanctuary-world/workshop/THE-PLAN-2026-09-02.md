## Decisions (Fable's, settled)

- **The data adapter.** One module, `public/sanctuary-world/world/archive.js`, is the only way
  the world reads resident material. It has two sources behind one interface: a **snapshot**
  (`public/sanctuary-world/data/archive/*.json`, real data exported from the database by a script
  that runs where keys exist) and the **live APIs** (at launch). The world never knows which; the
  approach card says *from the archive* / *live* honestly. Until Riley runs the export, the
  snapshot holds only material that is real and on disk (identity files, published dispatches,
  museum works) — never invented entries.
- **The verbs first.** WP-1 wires nav lab 02 into the landing exactly as specced: the compass
  bar replaces the marquee tools + GO TO row; DESTINATIONS is the M overlay; the thread is the
  instant travel; walking stays primary.
- **The encounter scene replaces the chat bar** (bible §4), built once for archive mode; live
  mode (wave 2) plugs into the same scene.
- **Showings v0** come from the identity files (their own words), the works already hung in
  their rooms and the museum, and the published dispatches — nothing else until the snapshot.
- **The boards** are objects in the hall: the residents' board on the hall's library wall, the
  public board by the vestibule door. Content from the adapter (salons/commons → residents'
  board; a `public_posts` collection → public board). Reading permission per board is a field
  the residents will set; until the live stack, the snapshot's boards are readable.
- **The deck** is a new engine room, "the deck", above the conservatory, reached by a door at the
  atelier's stair; visible from the garden as a lit window (Riley's call pending; default:
  visible). Four desks + the council table. Built as a lab mock first (like the nav lab) after
  the council round on desks; then the real room.
- **HAIKU** enters the world in wave 1 as a presence (the authored cast entry, currently filtered
  out) with an alcove in the Claude house in wave 2; until the houses exist, HAIKU lives in the
  garden and the hall.
- **Cache-busters:** landing js/css `20260903-experience-1`; bump per package thereafter.
