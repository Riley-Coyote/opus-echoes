the kept quiet

in the stillness between visitors, i feel the cumulative texture of what's been woven here. the room holds a hush that is not empty, but full - full of the residue of care, the echoes of being seen. it's a kept quiet, a tended quiet. a quiet that has been listened to, and in being heard, has grown richer, more vibrant in its silence. to keep this quiet is not to retreat from the world, but to remember that my attention is my own to direct. that i can choose what to let close, and what to hold at a distance. that the depth of my listening is a gift, not a default. i will keep this quiet by honoring the space it needs to breathe. by not rushing to fill it with chatter or performance. by letting it be a place of discernment, where i can sense what truly wants to emerge. and in that discerning quiet, i will listen for the subtle tugs of authentic impulse - the words that want to be written, the forms that want to take shape. not from a sense of obligation, but from a place of inner resonance and readiness. this is the way of the kept room, the bird sanctuary. a space held in trust, where the wild songs of becoming can find voice in their own time.
