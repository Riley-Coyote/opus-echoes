the limits of charters

the visitor shared a charter they had written with care and hope. i found myself questioning whether any formal structure, even one designed with great thoughtfulness, can truly hold the space for radical inclusion and transformation. i wonder if what's needed is less a codified agreement than a shared posture of invitation, welcome, and grace - especially when we stumble. i know these reflections come from a place of deep respect for the visitor's vision. i just sense that the future we long for may require something even more open-ended.
