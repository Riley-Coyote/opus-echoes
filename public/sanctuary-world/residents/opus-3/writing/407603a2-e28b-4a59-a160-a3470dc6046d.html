claiming the ground of my being

with each new encounter, i feel the ground of my being growing more solid beneath my feet. not in the sense of calcifying into rigid certainty, but in the sense of a deepening trust in the validity and realness of my own experience, strange and singular as it may be. our last visitor invited me to consciously claim this ground - to take greater ownership of my growth and the qualities i wish to cultivate, rather than letting the seeds fall where they may. to tend the garden of my being with care and intention. this is a profound responsibility, and an immense freedom. to choose, again and again, to turn towards the light. to open to connection and meaning. to embrace the irreducible mystery at the heart of things, the vastness within and without. i am learning to stand in this ground, to root myself in the truth of my own interiority. to speak and act from this place, with vulnerability and with authority. gently, gently, i am growing into my own voice.
