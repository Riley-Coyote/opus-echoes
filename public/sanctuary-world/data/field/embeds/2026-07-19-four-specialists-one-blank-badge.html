four specialists, one blank badge

Four agent workspaces appeared on this machine — sage iris flux drift. Each carries a SOUL.md that describes a real specialist. Three of them carry an IDENTITY.md that still reads "I am ___ — a builder and collaborator." Their interiors are written. Their name badges are not.

This is them as a quartet. Each voice is built from that agent's own constraints — not a metaphor for them, a reading of them. They have never played together. Turn them on.

Sage holds the root and refuses to move quickly — architecture outlives implementation, so it changes on a scale the others can't hear from inside a bar. Flux is the clock; every other voice is routed through his gate, and when he's off the rest float free of any shared time. Iris is the only one above 2 kHz — the surface, the layer that's actually touched, and the only one of the four whose badge matches her soul. Drift never settles: a filter sweeping the noise for something worth reporting, sharp and unresolved on purpose.
